About FIT//OS

Health should feel like an operating system.

Not ten apps, ten logins and ten subscriptions. One calm, fast place where your meals, workouts, habits, vitals and goals finally talk to each other.

🎯

Our mission

Make consistent healthy living effortless — by removing friction from tracking and giving you clear, honest feedback on your own data.

🔭

Our vision

A single personal health OS that works for everyone, in any language and on any device, from a low-end phone to a desktop.

🇧🇩

Built in Bangladesh

Designed with local life in mind — local meals, local payment methods (bKash & Nagad) and a price that makes sense.

Principles

What we will never compromise on.

🔒

Private by default

Your account is yours alone. Ingredients and entries you add are never shared with other users — only the curated main library is shared, read-only.

⚡

Fast by design

A single lightweight app that opens instantly, logs in seconds and keeps working offline. No bloat, no waiting.

🧩

One connected system

Nutrition feeds goals, habits feed streaks, vitals feed trends. Everything shares one source of truth.

🤝

Honest pricing

Free to start. Pro is a simple, transparent plan you pay for locally — and you get access the moment you submit.

Journey

From a personal tracker to a full platform.

Foundation

The core tracker

Nutrition, workouts, habits, goals, vitals and measurements in a single fast web app with cloud sync.

Growth

Libraries & intelligence

A shared main ingredient library, personal libraries per user, an AI Agent and a full admin console.

Now

Installable, Pro-ready

Install as a Progressive Web App, automatic update notices, and Pro subscriptions with bKash and Nagad.

Next

Deeper insight

Richer reports, more Pro features and more ways to pay — shaped by what members ask for.
